This is a sample feature with the text on the left page and a photo on the right. Choose it in the admin panel by setting the left page to Text and the right page to Photo.

The title, the credit and the text sit together on one page, which makes this layout good for short features, captions that grow into stories, or an introduction to a photo series.

## A short subhead

Subheads work here too. If the text is longer than the page, it is cut at the bottom, so keep it to a few paragraphs.
