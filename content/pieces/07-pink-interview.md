Q: This is a sample question on a pink spread. What draws you to color?

A: This is a sample answer. Color sets the mood of a spread before anyone reads a word, so choosing it is part of the editing.

Q: How do you decide which pieces get a color?

A: Usually the ones that need a pause or a change of pace. A colored spread between two neutral ones feels like turning a corner.

Q: Any advice for someone starting their own magazine?

A: Start small, keep a clear grid and let each issue have one surprise.
