This is sample text for an essay. Replace it from the admin panel or edit this file. Paragraphs are separated by a blank line and flow through two columns, the way long reads are set in an independent magazine.

A good essay page gives the reader room. The title sits alone on the left page, set very large, while the text runs on the facing page in narrow columns that are comfortable to follow. The credit runs vertically along the outer edge.

Use this layout for longer writing: reviews, opinion pieces, critical texts or anything that needs a few hundred words. If the text is longer than the page, it is cut at the end of the second column, so keep each piece to a single spread.

## I. A subhead

Start a line with two hash marks and a space to set a subhead in the large serif. Subheads help break a long text into parts and give the page a rhythm.

The colors of this spread are set on the piece itself. Any piece can have its own page and text colors, and the rest of the magazine keeps the colors chosen in the design settings.
