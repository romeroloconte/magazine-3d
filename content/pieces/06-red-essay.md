This is sample text on a red spread. Strong colors work best with short, confident pieces: a manifesto, an editor's letter, a list of ideas.

When a page is this loud, keep the type quiet. One weight, generous margins and plenty of space between paragraphs let the color do the talking.

## II. Contrast

White text on red reads well at this size. If you choose a lighter color for the page, switch the text back to black from the admin panel.

Every spread can have its own palette, so an issue can move from calm, neutral pages to a burst of color and back again.
